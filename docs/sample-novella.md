# The Last Light in the Library

*A small fictional excerpt, for the Literary reading theme.*

## Chapter One

By the time Mira reached the old library, the rain had softened into the kind of mist that seemed to belong to the streets themselves.

The windows were dark except for one. On the top floor, where nobody had worked for years, a single amber light hung in the silence.

> It was not the light that unsettled her. It was the certainty that someone had been waiting for her to see it.

She took the steps two at a time, holding her coat close, and pushed against the heavy wooden door. It opened with an exhausted sigh.

### An unexpected letter

On the caretaker's desk lay an envelope. There was no stamp, no address, and no name. Only a short instruction in ink:

*Begin where the stories end.*

She looked toward the stairs. Somewhere above her, a floorboard answered.

---

## Chapter Two

The narrow staircase smelled of cedar and old paper. At its end was a room that had never appeared on any map of the building.

Mira entered without turning on the light. The books around her seemed to be holding their breath.
